02 / About me

A curiosity
for the
unseen.

I study how soil microbial communities respond to fire and other environmental stressors.

I am a Ph.D. student in Microbiology and Molecular Biology at the University of Idaho. My work uses molecular approaches, including gene-expression analysis and plasmid-based DNA barcoding, to investigate microbial responses to environmental change.

My research experience spans the United States and Russia, with a background in biology, biochemistry, biophysics, and programming. I have a soft spot for tiny microbes and the big stories they can tell.
